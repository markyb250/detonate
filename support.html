How to play

Get the cover off
Tap each screw. Some screws are stuck and need a second tap.
Find the detonator
Look for the flashing red light on the right and follow its wire back to the left.
Cut it
Tap the wire inside the yellow CUT zone. With more than one detonator, cut them in number order: 1, then 2, then 3.
New to it?
Open "How to play" on the home screen and tap "Play the training bomb". The clock is stopped and nothing can blow up.
In the dark
On Extreme the lights are out, so move your finger to shine the torch. On Impossible the power keeps cutting out and the torch battery runs down, then recharges when the lights come back.

Common questions

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Your coins, tools and best times are saved only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them too.
There's no sound
Check that your device isn't on silent, and turn the volume up with the buttons on the side.
What is free, and what does the full game unlock?
Easy and Medium, the daily bomb, duels on Easy and Medium and playing a friend's bomb are free. One payment unlocks the full game for good: Hard, Extreme and Impossible, Endless, the weekly challenge and the bomb builder. There are no adverts and no subscription.
I bought the full game but it's locked
Tap anything with a padlock, then "Restore purchase". Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. With Family Sharing, the buyer needs purchase sharing switched on.
What is the weekly challenge?
Five bombs in a row with one twist that changes every week, the same for every player. One explosion ends the run. You get three tries a week that count, and after that you can practise. The week starts on Monday.
How does a duel work?
On the home screen, tap "Choose your game", pick "Duel", then "Set up a duel". Pick a difficulty and how many rounds, then pass the phone. Each round both players defuse a bomb in the same city. The faster player wins the round, and blowing up loses it. If the scores are level at the end, it goes to sudden death. Tools are switched off, and duels don't earn coins or change your scores.
How do I earn coins?
A city pays its full reward the first time you defuse it, and a quarter on replays. Faster defusals add a speed bonus (two and three stars). You also earn coins for a daily bomb streak, for each promotion, and for finishing the weekly challenge. Coins can't be bought, and you can carry three of each tool.
What are looks?
Looks are bomb casings, torch colours and explosion styles you buy with coins in the Shop. They only change how the game looks, never how a bomb plays, so they stay on in every mode. Tap "Use" in the Shop to switch between the ones you own.
What are ranks and kit?
Your Missions score and your best Endless run add up to career points, which earn real British Army ranks, from Private up to Major. Each rank unlocks a kit upgrade in the Shop that you buy once with coins. Kit doesn't count in the daily bomb, the weekly challenge or workshop bombs, so everyone is on level terms there.
How do I play a friend's bomb?
On the home screen, tap "Choose your game", pick "Workshop", then "Open the workshop" and "Enter a friend's code". Type or paste the code they sent you (it starts with DTN). You get the same bomb they built, and their time to beat if they set one.
How do I send someone my bomb?
On the home screen, tap "Choose your game", pick "Workshop", then "Open the workshop". Build a bomb and tap "Get the code". The game shows a code you can share or copy. To set a time for your friend to beat, tap "Play it first" and defuse it yourself. Your bombs are listed under "My bombs" so you can share one again later.
Does it need the internet?
No. Detonate plays fully offline. Only the leaderboard needs a connection.
How does the leaderboard work?
Every bomb you defuse adds to your score, with more points for harder difficulties and faster times. In Endless mode, the score is for one run of bombs that get harder as you go, and the board keeps your best run. The Daily bomb is the same for everyone each day: you get one go that counts, with tools switched off. Tap "Leaderboard" to compare with other players. It uses Apple's Game Center, so you need to be signed in to Game Center in your device's Settings. Your name on the board is your Game Center nickname.
