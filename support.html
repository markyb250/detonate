How to play

Get the cover off
Tap each screw. Some screws are stuck and need a second tap.
Find the detonator
Look for the flashing red light on the right and follow its wire back to the left.
Cut it
Tap the wire inside the yellow CUT zone. With more than one detonator, cut them in number order: 1, then 2, then 3.
New to it?
Open "How to play" on the home screen and tap "Play the training bomb". The clock is stopped and nothing can blow up.
In the dark
On Extreme the lights are out, so move your finger to shine the torch. On Impossible the power keeps cutting out and the torch battery runs down, then recharges when the lights come back.

Common questions

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Your coins, tools and best times are saved only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them too.
There's no sound
Check that your device isn't on silent, and turn the volume up with the buttons on the side.
What is the weekly challenge?
Five bombs in a row with one twist that changes every week, the same for every player. One explosion ends the run. You get three tries a week that count, and after that you can practise. The week starts on Monday.
What are ranks and kit?
Your Missions score and your best Endless run add up to career points, which earn real British Army ranks, from Private up to Major. Each rank unlocks a kit upgrade in the Shop that you buy once with coins. Kit doesn't count in the daily bomb, the weekly challenge or workshop bombs, so everyone is on level terms there.
How do I play a friend's bomb?
On the home screen, open "Bomb workshop" and tap "Enter a friend's code". Type or paste the code they sent you (it starts with DTN). You get the same bomb they built, and their time to beat.
How do I send someone my bomb?
In "Bomb workshop", build a bomb and defuse it yourself. The game then shows a code you can share or copy. Your bombs are listed under "My bombs" so you can share one again later.
Does it need the internet?
No. Detonate plays fully offline. Only the leaderboard needs a connection.
How does the leaderboard work?
Every bomb you defuse adds to your score, with more points for harder difficulties and faster times. In Endless mode, the score is for one run of bombs that get harder as you go, and the board keeps your best run. The Daily bomb is the same for everyone each day: you get one go that counts, with tools switched off. Tap "Leaderboard" to compare with other players. It uses Apple's Game Center, so you need to be signed in to Game Center in your device's Settings. Your name on the board is your Game Center nickname.
