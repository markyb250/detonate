Detonate – Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

In short: Detonate does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts of its own, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The only time it uses the internet is for the optional leaderboard, which is run by Apple's Game Center.

What the game saves

To remember your progress, Detonate saves a small amount of game data on your device: your coins, the tools you own, your best times for each city and your chosen difficulty. This data never leaves your device. We can't see it, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

Leaderboard (Apple Game Center)

Detonate has a leaderboard run by Apple's Game Center. It is optional: the game plays the same without it. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the game sends your total score to Apple so it can appear on the leaderboard beside your Game Center nickname. Apple holds that information under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive your name, your Apple Account or anything else about you. To stay off the leaderboard, sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings.

What we don't collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or contacts
  • No location
  • No photos, camera or microphone
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage statistics
  • No crash reports or analytics from third-party services

Third parties

Detonate contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services, and it doesn't share data with anyone, because it has none to share.

Children

Detonate is suitable for players aged 9 and over. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing. If purchases are added in a future version, they will be handled by Apple, and this policy will be updated first.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
